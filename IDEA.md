**Math Land**
An adaptive adventure game where players solve math puzzles to explore and restore a magical world.

- **Dynamic difficulty scaling** adjusts problem complexity based on player performance, keeping challenges engaging but achievable.
- **Story-driven progression** unlocks new regions and characters as players master arithmetic, geometry, and logic.
- **Visual puzzle integration** ties solutions to in-world actions, like rebuilding bridges by calculating angles or unlocking gates with pattern recognition.
- **Multiplayer co-op mode** allows friends to solve team-based challenges, fostering collaboration and communication.
- **Teacher dashboard** tracks student progress, pinpoints struggling skills, and generates printable practice sheets.
